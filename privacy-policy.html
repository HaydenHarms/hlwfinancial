Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

HLW Financial, LLC (“HLW Financial,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information in connection with our website at https://haydenharms.github.io/hlwfinancial/ (the “Site”) and our tax preparation, bookkeeping, and advisory services.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us:

  • Contact and intake form submissions (name, email, phone number, business name)
  • Newsletter or communication sign-ups
  • Information you provide when engaging us for services, including personal and financial information necessary to prepare tax returns or perform bookkeeping/advisory work (e.g., income documents, bank and account records, Social Security or EIN numbers, prior returns) — collected only through our secure client portal or other designated secure channel, not through general website forms

Information collected automatically:

  • Standard technical data (IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring URL) via server logs and any analytics tools we use
  • Cookies or similar technologies, if used, for site functionality and analytics (see Section 5)

2. How We Use Information

We use information to: respond to inquiries and schedule consultations; provide, manage, and bill for tax, bookkeeping, and advisory services under a signed engagement; maintain and secure our systems; comply with legal, licensing, and regulatory obligations (including IRS and Texas requirements applicable to a PTIN-registered preparer); and improve the Site.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With service providers who support our operations (e.g., our accounting/tax software providers, secure client portal, cloud storage, IT/security vendors), under obligations to protect it and use it only to provide services to us
  • With regulators or as required by law, subpoena, or legal process
  • In connection with a business transfer (e.g., merger or sale of the firm), subject to confidentiality obligations
  • With your consent, or as necessary to complete a service you request

Financial privacy notice (GLBA). As a firm providing tax preparation and related financial services, we are subject to the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act. We do not disclose nonpublic personal information about current or former clients to nonaffiliated third parties except as permitted by law — for example, to service providers who assist us in servicing your account, or as needed to complete a transaction you have requested.

We do not share client nonpublic personal information for third-party marketing purposes. Clients are not currently required to take any action to limit our sharing because we do not share for purposes that trigger an opt-out right under GLBA; if that changes, we will provide a revised notice describing your opt-out rights.

4. Data Security

We maintain a written information security program (“WISP”) designed to protect client information, consistent with the FTC Safeguards Rule, including administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The Site does not currently use cookies or third-party analytics tools. If that changes, this section will be updated to describe what is used and how you can control it (for example, through your browser settings).

6. Data Retention

We retain client records for the period required by IRS recordkeeping requirements, Texas law, and our professional obligations, and general website inquiry data for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us to ask about the information we hold about you, to update contact information, or to unsubscribe from communications. Depending on your state of residence, you may have additional rights (such as under the California Consumer Privacy Act) to know, delete, or limit certain uses of your personal information; contact us at harms.e.hayden@gmail.com to make such a request.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date above, and for existing clients, in the annual privacy notice we provide as required by GLBA.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices can be sent to harms.e.hayden@gmail.com or 5900 Balcones Drive, STE 100, Austin, TX 78731, USA.
