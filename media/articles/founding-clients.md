HLW Financial is opening a limited number of founding-client engagements at a discounted rate — for business owners willing to work closely with us while we build out the firm’s tools and workflows.

In practice, that means shorter feedback loops than a typical client relationship: you’ll see draft reports before they’re finalized, weigh in on how your dashboard is organized, and flag what’s confusing or missing as we go. Nothing experimental touches your actual filings — the discount is for your time and input, not for cutting corners on the work itself.

Spots are limited, and we’re prioritizing businesses whose books need to get current — bookkeeping, tax prep, or both. If that sounds like you, reach out through Get Started and mention you’re interested in the founding-client rate.
