HLW Financial QuickBooks Integration — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This application accesses QuickBooks Online data (transactions, accounts, reports) on behalf of HLW Financial, LLC solely to provide internal accounting, bookkeeping, and advisory services to the firm and its clients.

Data accessed through this integration is used only for these business purposes and is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party outside HLW Financial, LLC, except as required to deliver services to the client whose data is being accessed, or as required by law.

Data is transmitted and stored using encrypted connections. Access is limited to authorized HLW Financial personnel. Users may revoke this App's access to their QuickBooks Online account at any time through Intuit's account management settings.
